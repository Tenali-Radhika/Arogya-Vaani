# Pre-visit preparation
- Blood sugar (fasting) test: do not eat or drink anything except water for 8 to 10 hours before the test. Take usual medicines only if the doctor has said so.
- Bring all old reports, X-rays, scans and your current medicine strips or the prescription to every visit.
- Orthopaedics (knee, back, joint pain): bring old X-ray or MRI films. Wear loose, comfortable clothing that lets the doctor see the knee or joint.
- Cardiology visits: bring the latest ECG and list of heart medicines. Do not skip your morning tablets unless advised.
- Children (paediatrics): bring the vaccination card and note the child's temperature readings.
- Pregnancy (gynaecology): bring the mother and child protection card and previous scan reports.
- Arrive 10 minutes early with a photo ID. Carry your Ayushman card if you have one.
