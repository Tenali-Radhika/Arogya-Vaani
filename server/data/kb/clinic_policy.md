# Clinic policies
- Appointments are held for 15 minutes; please arrive on time.
- Cancel or reschedule at least 2 hours before the slot at no charge through ArogyaVaani.
- Consultation fee is paid at the clinic counter unless a payment link is sent.
- A follow-up visit within 7 days of the first consultation is usually at a reduced fee.
- Emergency: for chest pain, trouble breathing, stroke signs or heavy bleeding call 108 immediately. Do not wait for an appointment.
- ArogyaVaani gives guidance only and never a diagnosis. The doctor decides treatment.
