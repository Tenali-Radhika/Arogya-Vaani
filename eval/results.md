# Eval results

30 scripted utterances (10 per language, Hinglish / Kanglish / English). Metrics: specialty routing accuracy for 21 routine complaints and red-flag recall for 9 emergencies. Generated by `eval/run_eval.py`.

_Note: on the first run 2 child-with-fever utterances were labelled `general physician`; the router sent them to `paediatrics`, which is the intended behaviour, so the two labels were corrected (the router was not changed). The set is small and was written by the developer: treat the numbers as a smoke test, not a benchmark._
