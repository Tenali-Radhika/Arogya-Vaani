# PM-JAY / Ayushman Bharat basics
- PM-JAY (Ayushman Bharat) gives eligible families health cover of up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year for hospital care at empanelled hospitals.
- Eligibility depends on the family being listed in the SECC database or state scheme. Carry your Ayushman card or Aadhaar and ration card.
- Cover is for hospital admissions; routine OPD consultations are often not covered. Ask the clinic desk.
- Check eligibility and the list of empanelled hospitals on the official website https://pmjay.gov.in or call the helpline 14555.
- In Karnataka, the scheme works together with Arogya Karnataka. Doctors marked PM-JAY empanelled in ArogyaVaani are demo data only.
